📌 What Is DIGI-LEAD-HUNTER?

DIGI-LEAD-HUNTER is a Windows application that searches for local businesses in your chosen area and turns them into an organised list of leads you can actually use. Instead of spending hours copying business names, phone numbers and websites into a spreadsheet by hand, you let the software do the repetitive work while you get on with selling.

The application is described as an autonomous B2B local lead finder and blueprint generator. In plain language, that means two things:

1. It finds leads. It looks for local businesses in a category and location you specify — for example, cafés in Manchester or plumbers in Leeds — and collects their public business details into a tidy list.

2. It builds a blueprint. Rather than dumping a raw list on you, it generates a structured plan that helps you decide who to contact first and what angle to use.

The software is multi-threaded, which simply means it works on several searches at the same time instead of one after another. That makes it noticeably quicker than doing the work manually. It is published as an open-source tool by DigiFormation Ltd, a company registered in the United Kingdom under number 16994903.

🖥️ Before You Start

DIGI-LEAD-HUNTER is built for Windows. To get the best experience, make sure your computer meets the following:

  • A Windows 10 or Windows 11 computer, 64-bit.
  • Around 2 GB of free disk space so the application and its saved results have room to work.
  • At least 4 GB of memory — 8 GB is more comfortable for larger searches.
  • A stable internet connection, since the tool needs to look up businesses online.
  • Permission to install software on the computer you are using. If it is a work laptop, you may need to ask your IT team first.

You do not need to install Python, a code editor, or any developer tools. The application is provided in a ready-to-run form for Windows users.
